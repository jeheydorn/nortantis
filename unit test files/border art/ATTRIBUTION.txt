borders/rose

The two images in borders/rose are taken unmodified from the FA01-Temperate art pack:
https://cartographyassets.com/assets/96299/fa01-temperate/

That pack is released under the CAL-NR license ("No restrictions. Do whatever you want!"), which
permits this use. They are included here because they are the art that motivated the soft-inner-edge
border work: the inner edge of rose/top_edge.png is scalloped, so a quarter of the band's width along
its map-facing side is transparent. Nortantis' own installed borders are all opaque along that side,
so without this art there is nothing in the repository that exercises the feature on real artwork.

Used by BorderRevealTest.
